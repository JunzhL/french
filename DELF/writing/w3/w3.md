# French Writing Plan — File 3
## Week 3: Stronger Sentence Structure and More Natural Paragraphs

This file is for your **third week of French writing practice**.

Your Week 2 writing shows clear progress:
- you wrote longer and more detailed paragraphs
- your ideas are easier to follow
- you are starting to use more structure in descriptive and opinion writing

At the same time, Week 2 also showed some recurring issues:
- infinitive structures after prepositions and verbs
- article and preposition choice
- sentence building that follows English too closely
- agreement and spelling
- awkward phrasing in longer opinion paragraphs

So Week 3 will focus on making your sentences **more stable**, **more French**, and **better connected**.

---

## Main goal of Week 3

By the end of this week, you should be able to:
- write paragraphs of **90 to 130 words**
- build clearer multi-sentence paragraphs
- use more natural French sentence patterns
- reduce mistakes with infinitives and connectors
- describe places, habits, and opinions with better flow

This week focuses on:
- infinitive structures
- sentence connection
- description with relative clauses
- opinion writing with clearer logic
- rewriting weak sentences into natural French

---

## Daily structure for this week

Use this structure each day.

### 1. Review — 10 to 15 minutes
Review:
- 5 corrected sentences from Week 2
- 5 verbs
- 3 structures
- 3 repeated mistakes

### 2. Controlled practice — 10 minutes
Write:
- 5 to 8 short model-based sentences using the day's grammar focus

### 3. Main writing task — 20 to 25 minutes
Write one paragraph of **90 to 130 words**.

### 4. Self-check — 10 minutes
Before sending:
- check verbs
- check infinitives
- check articles/prepositions
- check agreement
- check spelling and accents
- check whether the sentence sounds too directly translated from English

### 5. Rewrite — 10 minutes
After correction:
- rewrite 3 weak sentences
- rewrite 1 paragraph in a cleaner version

---

## Rules for Week 3

1. Keep your ideas clear and specific.
2. Prefer natural French phrasing over word-for-word translation.
3. Build shorter correct sentences first, then combine them.
4. Reuse strong patterns from correction.
5. Pay special attention to infinitive structures.

## Day 1 — Infinitive structures that you keep using

### Goal
Fix the most common infinitive-related mistakes from Week 2.

### Grammar focus
- **avant de + infinitif**
- **pour + infinitif**
- **apprendre à + infinitif**
- **permettre de + infinitif**
- **consacrer du temps à + infinitif**
- **essayer de + infinitif**

### Controlled practice
Write one sentence for each:
- avant de ...
- pour ...
- j'apprends à ...
- cela permet de ...
- je consacre du temps à ...
- j'essaie de ...

### Main task
Write **90 to 110 words** about your weekday evening routine.

### Required expressions
Use all of these:
- **avant de**
- **pour**
- **après le dîner**
- **je consacre ... à ...**
- **j'essaie de**

### Self-check
Check:
- did I use an infinitive after **pour**?
- did I write **avant de** correctly?
- did I accidentally use a conjugated verb where an infinitive is needed?

## Day 2 — Describing a place more naturally

### Goal
Write a more natural descriptive paragraph about a real place.

### Grammar focus
- **il y a**
- **on trouve**
- **se situer**
- **au centre de**
- **au nord de / à l'ouest de**
- **près de**
- simple relative clauses:
  - **qui**
  - **où**
  - **dont** (only if you feel comfortable)

### Controlled practice
Write 6 short sentences describing a campus, city, or neighborhood.

### Main task
Write **100 to 120 words** describing your campus or another place you know well.

### Required expressions
Use at least 4 of these:
- **il y a**
- **on trouve**
- **au centre de**
- **près de**
- **qui**
- **où**
- **se trouve / est situé**
- **en général**

### Self-check
Check:
- did I describe the place instead of just listing facts?
- did I use location expressions correctly?
- did I connect my sentences smoothly?

## Day 3 — Better people description and personal profile writing

### Goal
Describe yourself or another person with more variety and better agreement.

### Grammar focus
- adjectives for personality and habits
- **être** vs **avoir**
- possessives
- relative clause with **qui**
- expressions of time:
  - **depuis**
  - **quand j'avais ... ans**
  - **dès l'âge de ...**

### Controlled practice
Write:
- 3 sentences about yourself
- 3 sentences about someone else

### Main task
Write **100 to 120 words** about yourself as a student and person.

### Required expressions
Use at least 4:
- **je suis**
- **j'ai**
- **je fais partie de**
- **depuis**
- **quand j'avais**
- **j'aime**
- **qui**

### Self-check
Check:
- article + noun + adjective agreement
- verb tense choice
- whether each sentence adds something new

## Day 4 — Preferences with stronger logic

### Goal
Write an opinion paragraph that is better connected and less repetitive.

### Grammar focus
- **à mon avis**
- **je pense que**
- **d'abord**
- **ensuite**
- **de plus**
- **cependant**
- **en conclusion**
- **non seulement..., mais aussi...**

### Controlled practice
Write 6 opinion sentences:
- 2 with **je pense que**
- 1 with **non seulement..., mais aussi...**
- 1 with **cependant**
- 1 with **de plus**
- 1 conclusion sentence

### Main task
Write **100 to 120 words** on one of these:
- Pourquoi la musique est-elle importante ?
- Pourquoi le sport est-il utile ?
- Est-il important d'avoir des loisirs ?
- Les technologies améliorent-elles la vie quotidienne ?

### Paragraph structure
1. opinion
2. reason 1
3. reason 2
4. added point
5. contrast or limit
6. conclusion

### Self-check
Check:
- did I explain my reasons clearly?
- did I overuse **j'aime**?
- did I use connectors correctly?

## Day 6 — Stronger guided B2-style opinion paragraph

### Goal
Write your clearest opinion paragraph so far.

### Topic choices
Choose one:
- Est-il important d'apprendre plusieurs langues ?
- Préférez-vous vivre en ville ou à la campagne ?
- Est-il préférable d'étudier seul ou avec d'autres ?
- Les voyages sont-ils importants pour les jeunes ?

### Target length
**110 to 140 words**

### Required structure
1. introduction of opinion
2. reason 1
3. reason 2
4. example or practical consequence
5. contrast or limit
6. conclusion

### Required language
Use all of these:
- **À mon avis**
- **D'abord**
- **Ensuite**
- **De plus**
- **Cependant**
- **En conclusion**
- **non seulement..., mais aussi...**
- **permettre de + infinitif**

### Self-check
Check:
- is my paragraph logically ordered?
- did I write natural French rather than translated English?
- are the verb forms stable?
