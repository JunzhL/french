## 2. Main repeated error categories

## A. Verb conjugation and verb form choice
This was one of the biggest recurring issues.

### Typical problems
- wrong present tense form
- wrong past form
- infinitive used instead of a conjugated verb
- wrong helper structure after another verb
- missing **de** before infinitive in some expressions

### Examples
- **je me sortis** → **je sors**
- **je finis travailler** → **je finis de travailler**
- **je suis en train de apprendre** → **je suis en train d'apprendre**
- **ils reprendient leur retraite** → **ils ont pris leur retraite**
- **ils sont devenant** → **elles deviennent**
- **quand on se stressé** → **quand on est stressé**
- **on peut soulagez** → **on peut soulager**

### What to review
- present tense of common verbs
- infinitive vs conjugated verb
- common patterns like:
  - **finir de + infinitif**
  - **être en train de + infinitif**
  - **pouvoir + infinitif**
  - **aller à / aller au**
- introduction to **passé composé**

---

## B. Articles and prepositions
This was another major pattern across nearly every day.

### Typical problems
- using the wrong article
- forgetting an article
- using English-style prepositions
- confusion with **à**, **au**, **en**, **de**, **du**, **des**

### Examples
- **de l'université** → **à l'Université de Waterloo**
- **à l'appartement à côte du campus** → **dans un appartement près du campus**
- **un verre du lait** → **un verre de lait**
- **à travail** → **au travail**
- **sur les weekends** → **le week-end**
- **dans le campus** → **sur le campus**
- **de anglais** → **d'anglais**
- **beaucoup des plats** → **beaucoup de plats**

### What to review
- place prepositions: **à, au, en, dans, sur, près de, chez**
- article patterns:
  - **de + noun**
  - **beaucoup de**
  - **un verre de**
  - **jouer à** for sports/games
  - **jouer de** for instruments

## C. Gender and agreement
You often had the right idea, but agreement was inconsistent.

### Typical problems
- wrong noun gender
- adjective not matching noun
- plural not marked correctly
- pronoun agreement not matching noun gender/number

### Examples
- **de la saxophone** → **du saxophone**
- **plats localaux** → **plats locaux**
- **une grand musée** → **un grand musée**
- **ils** for **les oies** → **elles**
- **les gens qui ses rapproche** → **les gens qui s'approchent**

### What to review
- masculine/feminine noun memorization
- plural adjective endings
- agreement with **il / elle / ils / elles**
- agreement in descriptive writing

## D. Word choice and natural phrasing
This is where English influence was strongest.

### Typical problems
- direct translation from English
- unnatural collocations
- using a technically understandable phrase that is not the normal French expression

### Examples
- **l'enfant seulment** → **enfant unique**
- **a couple years** idea → **quelques années**
- **opportunity sociale** → **occasion de socialiser**
- **visiter mes amis** → **rendre visite à mes amis**
- **une partie importante pour étudie** → **une partie importante de l'apprentissage**

### What to review
- learn chunks, not single words only
- collect useful French expressions from corrections
- compare your sentence with the corrected version and ask:
  - what words changed?
  - what structure changed?
  - what would I have translated literally from English?

---

## E. Sentence structure and linking
You have good ideas, but some sentences were built too much like English.

### Typical problems
- sentence pieces joined incorrectly
- missing connectors or wrong connector structure
- word order problems
- overloaded sentence with too many ideas

### Examples
- **cette action non seulement fourni-on** should become:
  - **Cette façon d'étudier fournit non seulement..., mais aussi...**
- **les autres pouvent donner idées que on ne pense pas** should become:
  - **Les autres peuvent donner des idées auxquelles on ne pense pas.**

### What to review
- sentence frames:
  - **Je pense que...**
  - **À mon avis...**
  - **D'abord... Ensuite... Enfin...**
  - **non seulement..., mais aussi...**
- writing shorter sentences first, then combining them carefully

## F. Spelling, accents, and French word forms
This was frequent, but it is very normal for Week 1.

### Typical problems
- missing accents
- French spelling influenced by English
- phonetic spelling
- confusion in infinitive spellings

### Examples
- **préfére** → **préfère**
- **étuidier / étuider** → **étudier**
- **Géneralemment** → **Généralement**
- **ceries** → **cerises**
- **aggressives** → **agressives**
- **nievu** → **niveau**
- **artifacielle** → **artificielle**

### What to review
- accents in common verbs and adverbs
- correct spelling of your most-used words
- infinitives you use often:
  - **étudier**
  - **apprendre**
  - **regarder**
  - **socialiser**
  - **approcher**
