# French Writing Plan — File 1
## Week 1: Building Your First Writing Habits

This file is for your **first week of French writing practice**.

Your current starting point:
- You are a beginner.
- You are preparing for **B2/C1 DELF-style writing** in the long term.
- You have **never written in French before**.

So this week is **not** about writing advanced essays.
It is about building the habits and foundations that will let you write correctly later.

---

## Main goal of Week 1

By the end of this week, you should be able to:
- write simple French sentences
- introduce yourself in writing
- describe basic facts about your life
- connect a few ideas together
- notice your most common mistakes

This week focuses on:
- sentence structure
- present tense
- articles
- adjective agreement
- basic connectors
- short paragraph writing

---

## Daily structure for this week

Use this structure each day.

### 1. Review — 10 minutes
Review:
- yesterday's corrections
- 5 useful words
- 3 useful phrases
- 3 common verbs

### 2. Sentence practice — 10 minutes
Write **5 simple sentences** on the day's topic.

### 3. Main writing task — 15 to 20 minutes
Write **1 short paragraph** of about **50 to 80 words**.

### 4. Correction and error report — 10 to 15 minutes
After writing, identify:
- grammar mistakes
- verb mistakes
- article mistakes
- agreement mistakes
- word choice mistakes
- spelling/accent mistakes

### 5. Rewrite — 5 to 10 minutes
Rewrite:
- 3 corrected sentences
- or the full paragraph in a better version

---

## Rules for Week 1

1. Keep sentences short and clear.
2. Do not try to sound advanced.
3. Prioritize correctness over difficult vocabulary.
4. Reuse useful sentence patterns.
5. Always review your mistakes the next day.

## Day 1 — Introduce yourself

### Goal
Learn to write basic personal information in French.

### Grammar focus
- **être** — to be
- **avoir** — to have
- subject pronouns: je, tu, il, elle
- simple present tense
- articles: un, une
- basic adjective agreement

### Useful model phrases
- Je m'appelle ...
- Je suis étudiant / étudiante.
- J'ai ... ans.
- J'habite à ...
- J'aime ...
- Je parle ...
- Je veux apprendre le français.
- Mon objectif est de ...

### Task A — Sentence practice
Write 5 to 8 simple sentences about yourself.

Example topics:
- your name
- where you live
- what you study
- what languages you speak
- why you are learning French

### Task B — Main paragraph
Write a paragraph of **50 to 70 words** introducing yourself.

### Checklist before finishing
Check:
- Did each sentence have a verb?
- Did you use **je suis** and **j'ai** correctly?
- Did you choose the correct article?
- Did adjectives match masculine/feminine when needed?
- Did you avoid translating directly from English?

## Day 2 — Your daily life

### Goal
Write simple sentences about your everyday routine.

### Grammar focus
- present tense of common verbs
- reflexive routine expressions if needed
- time expressions
- frequency words

### Useful verbs
- **aller** — to go
- **faire** — to do / make
- **étudier** — to study
- **aimer** — to like
- **commencer** — to begin

### Useful phrases
- Tous les jours, ...
- Le matin, ...
- L'après-midi, ...
- Le soir, ...
- Je vais à ...
- J'étudie ...
- Je fais mes devoirs.
- En général, ...

### Task A — Sentence practice
Write 5 sentences about your normal day.

### Task B — Main paragraph
Write **60 to 80 words** describing your routine.

### Checklist
Check:
- Did you use time expressions?
- Did you conjugate verbs correctly?
- Did your paragraph follow a logical order?

### Error report
Use the same template as Day 1.

## Day 3 — Likes and dislikes

### Goal
Explain what you like, do not like, and why.

### Grammar focus
- **aimer**
- **préférer** — to prefer
- **détester** — to hate
- basic reasons with **parce que**
- simple contrast with **mais**

### Useful phrases
- J'aime ...
- Je n'aime pas ...
- Je préfère ...
- J'adore ...
- Je déteste ...
- parce que ...
- mais ...

### Task A — Sentence practice
Write 6 sentences about things you like and dislike.

### Task B — Main paragraph
Write **60 to 80 words** about your preferences.

### Goal for quality
This day is important because writing opinions is essential later for B2.

Try to give:
- one opinion
- one reason
- one contrast

Example structure:
- J'aime ...
- parce que ...
- Mais je n'aime pas ...
- Je préfère ...

## Day 4 — Family or friends

### Goal
Describe people.

### Grammar focus
- **avoir** and **être**
- adjective agreement
- describing age, personality, appearance
- possessive adjectives: mon, ma, mes

### Useful phrases
- J'ai un frère / une sœur.
- Mon ami est ...
- Ma mère est ...
- Il est ...
- Elle est ...
- Il a ...
- Elle a ...
- Nous sommes proches.

### Task A — Sentence practice
Write 5 to 7 sentences about a family member or friend.

### Task B — Main paragraph
Write **60 to 80 words** describing one person.

### Checklist
Check:
- Did you use **mon / ma / mes** correctly?
- Did your adjectives agree?
- Did you use **il est / elle est** and **il a / elle a** correctly?

## Day 5 — Your city, school, or environment

### Goal
Describe a place in simple French.

### Grammar focus
- **il y a** — there is / there are
- prepositions of place
- descriptive adjectives
- simple connectors

### Useful phrases
- Dans ma ville, il y a ...
- Mon école est ...
- C'est ...
- Il y a beaucoup de ...
- J'aime cet endroit parce que ...
- Cependant, ...
- Aussi, ...

### Task A — Sentence practice
Write 5 sentences about your city, school, neighborhood, or campus.

### Task B — Main paragraph
Write **70 to 80 words** describing that place.

### Checklist
Check:
- Did you use **il y a** correctly?
- Did you describe both positive and negative aspects?
- Did you connect ideas instead of making only a list?

## Day 6 — First guided opinion paragraph

### Goal
Write your first short opinion paragraph.

### Grammar focus
- opinion phrases
- cause and consequence
- connectors: parce que, donc, mais

### Possible prompts
Choose one:
- Pourquoi apprendre une langue étrangère est-il important ?
- Préférez-vous étudier seul ou avec d'autres ?
- Les téléphones sont-ils utiles pour les étudiants ?

### Paragraph structure to follow
Use exactly this structure:

1. **Opinion**
   - À mon avis, ...

2. **Reason 1**
   - D'abord, ...

3. **Reason 2**
   - Ensuite, ...

4. **Mini conclusion**
   - Donc / En conclusion, ...

### Task
Write **70 to 90 words**.

### This day matters
This is your bridge from basic writing to future DELF-style writing.
Do not worry about sounding advanced.
Focus on:
- clear structure
- correct verbs
- simple reasons
