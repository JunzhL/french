# French Writing Plan — File 2
## Week 2: Building Stronger Paragraphs and Better Accuracy

This file is for your **second week of French writing practice**.

Week 1 showed that you can already generate ideas and write short paragraphs.  
Now Week 2 will focus on making your writing **more correct, more natural, and more organized**.

Based on your Week 1 corrections, your biggest priorities now are:
- verb accuracy
- article and preposition choice
- agreement
- spelling and accents
- more natural French phrasing

---

## Main goal of Week 2

By the end of this week, you should be able to:
- write more accurate short paragraphs
- use common verbs with fewer mistakes
- build more natural French sentences
- explain ideas with clearer structure
- reduce literal translation from English

This week focuses on:
- present tense control
- irregular high-frequency verbs
- articles and prepositions
- adjective and noun agreement
- cleaner paragraph structure
- rewriting after correction

---

## Daily structure for this week

Use this structure each day.

### 1. Review — 10 to 15 minutes
Review:
- your Week 1 error report
- 5 verbs
- 5 expressions
- 3 repeated mistakes from last time

### 2. Controlled practice — 10 minutes
Write:
- 5 model-based sentences
- or transform 5 sentences using the grammar focus of the day

### 3. Main writing task — 20 minutes
Write one paragraph of **70 to 100 words**.

### 4. Self-check — 10 minutes
Before sending your work, check:
- verb endings
- articles
- gender/plural agreement
- accents
- sentence flow

### 5. Correction and rewrite — 10 to 15 minutes
After correction:
- rewrite 3 weak sentences
- write 1 improved version of the paragraph

---

## Rules for Week 2

1. Keep writing simple but cleaner.
2. Reuse corrected expressions from Week 1.
3. Prefer one correct sentence over one ambitious incorrect sentence.
4. Build paragraphs with clear logic.
5. Check verbs and prepositions before anything else.

## Day 1 — Fixing the most common verb patterns

### Goal
Improve the verb structures you used incorrectly in Week 1.

### Grammar focus
- **être** — to be
- **avoir** — to have
- **aller** — to go
- **faire** — to do / make
- **sortir** — to go out
- **finir de + infinitif**
- **être en train de + infinitif**
- **pouvoir + infinitif**

### Controlled practice
Write one sentence for each:
- je suis ...
- j'ai ...
- je vais ...
- je fais ...
- je peux ...
- je suis en train de ...
- je finis de ...

### Main task
Write a paragraph of **70 to 90 words** about your weekday routine.

### Extra requirement
You must correctly use:
- **je vais**
- **je fais**
- **je finis de**
- **pendant**
- **après**

### Self-check
Check especially:
- did you accidentally use an infinitive instead of a conjugated verb?
- did you forget **de** before the infinitive after some verbs?

## Day 2 — Articles and prepositions in real writing

### Goal
Reduce mistakes with **à, au, en, dans, sur, de, du, des**.

### Grammar focus
- **à / au / à la / aux**
- **en**
- **dans**
- **sur**
- **chez**
- **de / du / de la / des**
- quantity expressions with **de**

### Controlled practice
Write 8 short sentences using:
- à l'université
- au Canada
- dans un appartement
- sur le campus
- chez moi
- un verre de lait
- beaucoup de livres
- près de chez moi

### Main task
Write **80 to 100 words** describing your home, campus, or neighborhood.

### Extra requirement
Use correctly:
- **il y a**
- **beaucoup de**
- **près de**
- **dans**
- **sur**

### Self-check
Look for:
- missing articles
- English-style prepositions
- **de** after quantity expressions

## Day 3 — Describing people with better agreement

### Goal
Write more accurately about people.

### Grammar focus
- adjective agreement
- possessive adjectives: **mon, ma, mes**
- **il est / elle est**
- **il a / elle a**
- plural agreement

### Controlled practice
Write 6 sentences:
- 2 about a family member
- 2 about a friend
- 2 about yourself

### Main task
Write **80 to 100 words** describing one person who is important to you.

### Extra requirement
Include:
- personality
- age or life stage
- your relationship with that person
- at least 3 adjectives

### Self-check
Check:
- noun gender
- adjective endings
- possessive adjective choice
- pronoun agreement

### Grammar focus

- **parce que**

## Day 6 — Guided opinion paragraph, stronger version

### Goal
Write a clearer and more structured opinion paragraph than in Week 1.

### Topic choices
Choose one:
- Préférez-vous étudier seul ou avec d'autres ?
- Les étudiants devraient-ils utiliser leur téléphone en classe ?
- Est-il important d'apprendre plusieurs langues ?
- Vaut-il mieux vivre dans une grande ville ou une petite ville ?

### Target length
**90 to 120 words**

### Required structure
1. **Introduction of opinion**
   - À mon avis...
2. **Reason 1**
   - D'abord...
3. **Reason 2**
   - Ensuite...
4. **Counterpoint or contrast**
   - Cependant...
5. **Conclusion**
   - En conclusion...

### Required language
Use all of these:
- **je pense que**
- **parce que**
- **non seulement..., mais aussi...**
- **cependant**
- **en conclusion**

### Self-check
Check:
- did you keep the structure clear?
- did each sentence connect logically?
- did you avoid direct English translation?
